3. Website Infrastructure and Hosting

Abinod operates a primarily static website developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages, a static website hosting service provided by GitHub.

When you access the website, technical information is transmitted to the hosting infrastructure to deliver the requested content.

Depending on the request and hosting environment, this information may include:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested website resources
  • Browser and operating system information
  • Referring page information, where transmitted
  • Technical errors and security-related information

GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub.

Technical data necessary to deliver and protect the website is processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, reflecting our legitimate interest in operating a functional, reliable, and secure website.

15. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to the applicable requirements of the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about personal data we process concerning you (Article 15 GDPR).
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data (Article 16 GDPR).
  • Erasure: Request deletion of personal data where the legal conditions are met (Article 17 GDPR).
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances (Article 18 GDPR).
  • Data portability: Receive eligible personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (Article 20 GDPR).
  • Objection: Object to certain processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR).
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise your rights, please contact: contact@abinod.com .

We will respond in accordance with the applicable statutory deadlines.

16. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority if you believe your personal data has been processed unlawfully.

For a private-sector business based in Gelsenkirchen, the relevant supervisory authority is generally:

Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW)

Website: www.ldi.nrw.de

You may also contact another competent supervisory authority where permitted under the GDPR.
